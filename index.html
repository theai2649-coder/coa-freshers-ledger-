Simple rules. Shared trust.

Proposed process for organisers

01 / Record

Log each transaction

Record the date, amount, purpose and whether money came in or went out.

02 / Verify

Keep the evidence

Authorised organisers check receipts and have a second person review each entry.

03 / Publish

Share the final balance

Publish only approved totals, protect personal payment details, and agree what happens to any surplus.
